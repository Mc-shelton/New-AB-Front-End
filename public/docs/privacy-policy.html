Controller

Data We Collect

  • Identification and contact details such as name, email address, phone number, and postal address.
  • Registration information including event selected, ticket type, corral, and bib number.
  • Payment references such as transaction IDs and billing metadata received from our payment processor. We do not store full card details.

Why We Process Data (Lawful Basis)

  • To manage registration and deliver the event (performance of a contract).
  • To support safety, logistics, timing, and results (legitimate interests).
  • To send marketing communications where you have provided consent.
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations, including tax, accounting, and safety reporting.
  • To analyze usage and improve our services using minimized and proportionate data (legitimate interests).

Sharing

We share personal data only as necessary with trusted service providers for payment processing, email or app messaging, timing and results, customer support, and analytics. We may also disclose information to public authorities where legally required.

Retention

Registration and payment records are retained in accordance with applicable legal and accounting requirements. Device and diagnostic logs are rotated regularly. Marketing data is retained until you opt out.

International Transfers

Where personal data is transferred outside your jurisdiction, we apply appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent legal mechanisms.
