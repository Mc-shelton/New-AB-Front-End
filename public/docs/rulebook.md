# Rulebook

## Eligibility
- You are at least [18] on race day, medically fit, and agree to these rules and the Terms of Participation.

## Bib & Timing
- Wear your assigned bib on the front, unobstructed. No transfers, duplicates, or alterations.

## Course Conduct
- Stay on the marked course and follow all instructions from officials and volunteers.
- No pacing vehicles/bikes unless organizer‑authorized. No pets or strollers unless explicitly allowed.

## Safety
- Yield to medical staff; report hazards or incidents.
- Use official aid stations; remove headphones where prohibited by officials or local law.

## Fair Play
- No performance‑enhancing drugs; no unsporting behavior; obey local laws.

## Cut‑Offs
- Respect start times, corrals, and published time limits. Officials may remove participants for safety.

## Results
- Results are provisional until verified. Timing anomalies may be reviewed.

## Environment & Property
- Pack out trash; respect residents, businesses, and park/city rules.

## Media
- The event may film/photograph; you may appear in media without compensation.

## Violations
- Penalties include warnings, time penalties, disqualification, or bans. Officials’ decisions are final.
