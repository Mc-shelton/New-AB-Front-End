Rulebook

This Rulebook governs participation in the event. All participants are expected to read, understand, and comply with these rules at all times.

Eligibility

You must be at least 13 years old on race day, medically fit to participate, and have agreed to these rules and the Terms of Participation.

Bib & Timing

Your assigned bib must be worn visibly on the front of your body and remain unobstructed at all times. Bibs may not be transferred, duplicated, modified, or altered in any way.

Course Conduct

  • Remain on the marked course and obey all directions from officials and volunteers.
  • No pacing by vehicles, bicycles, or individuals unless explicitly authorized by the organizers.
  • Pets, strollers, or unauthorized equipment are not permitted unless explicitly allowed.

Safety

  • Yield immediately to medical staff and emergency responders.
  • Report hazards, injuries, or incidents to event officials.
  • Use official aid stations only and remove headphones where required by officials or applicable law.

Fair Play

The use of performance-enhancing drugs, unsporting behavior, or failure to comply with local laws is strictly prohibited.

Cut-Offs

Participants must adhere to published start times, corrals, and time limits. Officials may remove participants from the course for safety or rule compliance.

Results

All results are provisional until verified. Timing anomalies, rule violations, or discrepancies may be reviewed and corrected at the organizers’ discretion.

Environment & Property

Participants must dispose of waste responsibly, respect local residents and businesses, and comply with all park, city, or venue regulations.

Media

The event may be photographed or recorded. By participating, you acknowledge that you may appear in event media without compensation.

Violations

Violations may result in warnings, time penalties, disqualification, or future participation bans. Decisions by event officials are final.
